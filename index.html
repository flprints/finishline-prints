    Enquire

09 · Good to know

Printing questions, answered.

What does Finishline Prints do?

We are a creative printing studio in Royapettah, Chennai. We handle design and artwork, customized printing, large-format printing, pre-press preparation and post-press finishing such as binding and lamination.

Can you design my artwork, or should I bring my own?

Both work. We can create the design from your idea, or check, correct and prepare your existing artwork so it is print-ready.

Do you print banners and posters?

Yes. Our large-format printing covers posters, banners, display graphics and other promotional graphics.

Do you offer binding, lamination and folding?

Yes. Post-press and finishing includes binding, lamination, cutting and trimming, folding, and packaging and finishing.

Can you make customized calendars and diaries?

Yes. Customized calendars and diaries are part of our customized printing, alongside business stationery, corporate printing and promotional print materials.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form on this page, message us on WhatsApp, call +91 99448 83927 or email finishlineprint@gmail.com. Tell us what you need, the size, quantity and finish, and the date you need it by.

How long will my order take?

It depends on the product, the quantity and the finishing. Tell us your deadline when you ask for a quote and we will work towards delivering within the agreed timeline.
